SCENE 07 · DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

From requisition to first shift: a proposed governed sequence.

STEP 01 · REQUISITION

Manpower Planning

The planning step frames headcount, shift pattern, skill profile, site conditions and target timing for owner review.

STEP 02 · SOURCING

Sourcing & Screening

A governed recruitment design matches candidates to an approved position profile and requests checks only at the authorized stage.

STEP 03 · COMPLIANCE

Statutory Onboarding

Approved onboarding requirements, contracts and statutory records are completed through owned checkpoints before the first assigned shift.

STEP 04 · DEPLOYMENT

Site Orientation

The deployment plan defines orientation, safety briefing, site ownership and a clear escalation path for the operating team.

STEP 05 · CYCLE

Timekeeping & Payroll

Time records move through validation and approval gates before they become a payroll basis, with exceptions kept visible for the responsible owner.

STEP 06 · SUSTAIN

Performance & Retention

A governed review can bring attendance, turnover, employee relations and replacement planning into one owner-defined operating rhythm.
